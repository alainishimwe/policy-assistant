FICTIONAL AUTO INSURANCE POLICY
Policy number: DEMO-001
For portfolio demonstration only.

SECTION 1 — COLLISION COVERAGE
Collision coverage pays for covered damage to your vehicle
resulting from a collision. The collision deductible is $500.

SECTION 2 — COMPREHENSIVE COVERAGE
Comprehensive coverage pays for covered damage from theft,
fire, vandalism, and hail. The comprehensive deductible is $250.

SECTION 3 — RENTAL REIMBURSEMENT
Following a covered claim, rental reimbursement pays up to
$40 per day for a maximum of 30 days.

SECTION 4 — EXCLUSIONS
This sample policy excludes intentional damage and use of
the vehicle for paid rideshare services.

SECTION 5 — CLAIM DOCUMENTS
To submit a claim, provide your policy number, the incident
date, a description of the incident, and photos of the damage.
Provide a police report if one is available.
